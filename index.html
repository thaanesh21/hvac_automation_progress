A room feels warm. Where is the real problem?

Watch how the system will trace it back, one step at a time.

  1. RoomsSupply air reaching the room is warmer than it should be. The complaint is real.
  2. AHUCheck fan speed, valve opening and the temperature drop across the coil. This check is already live in the action centre.
  3. PumpsIs enough chilled water actually reaching this AHU, or is flow the problem?
  4. Chiller plantIs the water leaving the chiller cold enough? If yes, the cause is local. If not, it is the plant.

The answer lands on one machine, with evidence, so the right team goes to the right place the first time.

Four steps, each one building on the last

Value shows up early and grows with every step.

  1. 1

    Measure

    First screens live, rolling out now

    Run hours, efficiency rating and action centre for all connected AHUs, so everyone sees the same picture.

  2. 2

    Improve

    Next, at one pilot site

    Fan over-speed alerts and scheduling suggestions, tried first at a pilot site to prove the savings.

  3. 3

    Predict and trace

    After that

    Extend the screens to chillers, pumps and VRF, learn what normal looks like for each machine, and warn the team before a fault, pointing to its root cause.

  4. 4

    Automate

    Once proven and approved

    Let the system adjust equipment on its own in approved situations, like large offices cooled for full occupancy that are only partly used.

Safety first. No automatic control will be switched on until engineers have checked it and the site has approved it. Comfort and required room temperatures always come first.
